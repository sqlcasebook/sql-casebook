Support

SQL Casebook is an educational app for iPhone and iPad: 30 detective cases where every clue is uncovered with SQL queries against a real PostgreSQL database running on your device.

How to contact us

In the app: Profile → Support → Feedback. You can send a suggestion, a complaint or report an error in a task, and see the status of the reply.

By email: support@sql-casebook.com

Frequently asked questions

How do I restore purchased seasons?
Profile → Purchases → Restore purchases. Sign in to the App Store with the Apple ID you used for the purchase.
How do I get a refund?
Refunds are handled by Apple: go to reportaproblem.apple.com and select the purchase.
How do I delete my account?
Profile → Delete account. Your account, profile, progress and feedback are removed from the server immediately.
How do I get a copy of my data?
Profile → My data → Download my data. The app creates a JSON file.
How do I change the language?
Profile → Language and appearance. The device language is used by default.
Do I need an internet connection?
SQL queries run on your device. The internet is needed to sign in and sync your progress.
Which devices are supported?
iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17 or later.
